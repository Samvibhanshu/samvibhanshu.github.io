Hybrid real-time fall detection

A wearable that detects falls on the device itself and alerts caregivers.

  • IoT
  • 2026

What it does

A wearable fall detector that pairs a machine-learning model with physical checks: a fall is confirmed only when the model’s prediction and the acceleration and orientation thresholds agree, and then caregivers are alerted.

How it works

  • Model. A Random Forest trained on the SisFall dataset, using 3-second windows with 50% overlap.
  • Device. An edge-AI wearable built on an ESP32 with an MPU6050 motion sensor.
  • Confirmation. The model’s prediction is combined with acceleration and orientation thresholds before a fall is confirmed.
  • Alerting. Confirmed falls are sent to caregivers through the Blynk IoT platform.
